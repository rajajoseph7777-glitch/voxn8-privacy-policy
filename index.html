Privacy Policy

App: Falcon ERP (package com.voxn8.falconfield)
Developer: Voxn8
Effective date: 28 October 2026

Falcon ERP is a business app used by employees of companies that use the Falcon ERP system provided by Voxn8. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

1. Who can use the app

The app is for employees whose employer has given them a Falcon ERP account. There is no public sign-up. The app is not directed at children.

2. Data we collect

  • Account information – name, employee ID, phone/email and role, provided by your employer to sign you in.
  • Location (including background location) – only while a field employee has started their work day ("On duty"). The route is recorded so the employer can see field visits and calculate travel. Tracking stops when the employee ends the day. A visible "On duty" notification is shown the whole time.
  • Camera / photos – only when the user chooses to take a photo (for example a visit photo, ID card or gate pass).
  • Notifications – to alert users about gate passes, tasks and on-duty status.
  • Work records – out passes, gate entries, shop-floor, QC and maintenance entries made by the user in the app.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to run the employer's business operations in Falcon ERP: attendance and field-day tracking, gate passes, production and reporting. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except service providers that host the system for us.

4. Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secured servers operated by Voxn8 in India. Access is limited by user role.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the employer's Falcon ERP account is active or as required by law. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact your employer or write to us at the address below. Steps: Account & data deletion.

6. Your choices

You can deny or revoke location, camera and notification permissions at any time in your phone settings. Some features (such as field-day tracking) will not work without them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
