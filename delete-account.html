Account & Data Deletion

App: Falcon ERP (package com.voxn8.falconfield)
Developer: Voxn8

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@voxn8.com with the subject "Falcon ERP account deletion".
  2. Include your full name, company name, employee ID and the phone number or email used to sign in.
  3. We will verify the request with you or your employer and confirm by email.

You may also ask your employer's Falcon ERP administrator to deactivate and delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your login account and profile (name, phone, email, role).
  • Location / route history recorded during on-duty days.
  • Photos you uploaded from the app.
  • Device notification tokens.

What may be kept

Business records you created for your employer (gate passes, production, QC and maintenance entries, attendance) may be kept by the employer for up to 8 years where required for accounting, tax or legal reasons. Your name on these records can be anonymised on request.

Timeline

Requests are completed within 30 days. Backups are cleared within a further 30 days.

See also our Privacy Policy.
